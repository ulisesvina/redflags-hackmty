## TL;DR — adapt the edges, keep the core

The architecture we built is what the spec rewards, almost clause by clause: detectors make leads, an
investigator with tools proves or declines them, a validator gates every accusation, declined leads carry
reasons, everything is replayable from a log. **Nothing in `agent/` needs to be thrown away.**

What does not match is the **contract at the edges**, and one of those mismatches is fatal if ignored:

1. **Input.** Judges score us on estates *they* generate to `estate_schema.sql`, handed to us "at a path
   given at run time". Our loader, detectors and tools read *our* column names. On a judge estate we would
   load nothing and find nothing. Results = 0 regardless of our seed tables.
2. **Scheme enum.** Five fixed types: `phantom_vendor`, `kickback`, `round_tripping`, `threshold_splitting`,
   `revenue_inflation`. We cover three. `threshold_splitting` and `revenue_inflation` are new work;
   `duplicate_invoice_payment` is not a scheme type for them.
3. **Output.** A `submission.json` (prefixed entity ids, ≥3 exhibits each with `source_table`, a money
   trail, `confidence`, declined leads with `signal`/`tool_calls_made`/`closed_by`, `run_metadata` with LLM
   calls, MXN cost, wall-clock) **and** a case file with five required sections where the money trail is a
   **rendered diagram** (prose-only caps Clarity at 3). Our case file is a markdown table, our JSON is the
   scorer's shape, and we do not count LLM calls or cost.
4. **Amount rule.** `peso_amount` must equal the sum of cited exhibits, per table, within **2 %**. Our guard
   allows 25 % against a recomputed aggregate.

Decision: **adapter-first.** Make the judges' schema loadable into our existing `Dataset` today, export our
generator to their schema so our held-out numbers are on the same shape the judges use, then add the two
missing schemes and the output layer. The internal representation, the detector registry, the loop, the
guard and 230 tests stay.

## Schema mapping (judges' tables → our `Dataset`)

Their `bank_txns` has no `invoice_uuid`; payments link to invoices only through `reference` text and amount.
Their `employees` have **no address**, only `bank_clabe`. There is no `goods_receipts`, no `customers`, no
`counterparty_bank`; instead there are `purchase_orders` (with `approver`) and `contracts`.

| Judges' table.column | Ours | Adapter rule |
|---|---|---|
| `vendors.rfc` (PK), `legal_name`, `registered_date`, `address`, `bank_clabe`, `category` | `suppliers.supplier_id/name/onboarded/street+city/clabe/category` | `supplier_id = "RFC:<rfc>"`; `approved_by` from the majority PO approver |
| `invoices.uuid, issuer_rfc, receiver_rfc, issue_date, subtotal, iva, total, concepto_text, uso_cfdi, forma_pago, metodo_pago, status` | `invoices.*` | `tipo = recibida if receiver_rfc == company_rfc else emitida`; `counterparty_id = "RFC:<other rfc>"`; keep `status` (new) |
| `ledger.entry_id (int), date, account_code, account_name, debit, credit, description, invoice_uuid, cost_center, approver` | `ledger.*` | `entry_id` as str; `approver` (new) kept |
| `bank_txns.txn_id, date, from_clabe, to_clabe, amount, reference, channel` | `bank_transactions` + `counterparty_bank` | `direction = out/in` when from/to is the company CLABE, else the row becomes a `counterparty_bank` leg; `invoice_uuid` recovered from `reference` (`INV-…`/UUID) else by vendor CLABE + amount + date window; `channel` kept (efectivo = cash) |
| `purchase_orders.po_id, vendor_rfc, date, amount, requester, approver, description` | *(new frame)* + `goods_receipts` proxy | One `goods_receipts` row per invoice with a PO whose vendor and amount match (record id = `po_id`), so "no deliverable" detectors keep working; `ds.record_table[po_id] = "purchase_orders"` for exhibits |
| `contracts.contract_id, vendor_rfc, start_date, value, scope_text` | *(new frame)* | Used by the challenger and declined-lead checks (retainer/rent decoys) |
| `employees.emp_id ("EMP:0001"), name, role, bank_clabe, hire_date` | `employees` | No home address: the address-match detector is dead on judge data; kickback tell becomes CLABE-based |
| `efos_list.rfc, legal_name, status (definitivo/presunto), publication_date` | `efos_69b` | `situacion = status.capitalize()` |
| Company identity | `company.json` | Derive: `company_rfc` = receiver on most purchase invoices; `company_clabe` = most frequent `from_clabe` on those payments |

`agent.data.load(path)` auto-detects: our CSV dir, a judges' `.db`, or a judges' CSV dir. One entry point.

## Scheme mapping

| Judges' type | Ours | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `phantom_vendor` | `efos_fake_supplier` | Covered. Tell on judge data: `efos_list` RFC match, no PO/contract, fast pay, vague `concepto_text` |
| `kickback` | `kickback_shell` | Covered only via `detect_kickback_outflow` (bank_txns vendor CLABE → employee CLABE) and approver linkage on PO/ledger. Address match is unavailable. Decoy to expect: employee and vendor share the 3-digit bank code only |
| `round_tripping` | `round_trip_sales` | Covered when third-party legs exist in `bank_txns`. Add a 2-hop fallback: out to X, in from X (same CLABE or same RFC as vendor and customer) |
| `threshold_splitting` | — | **New.** Several invoices/POs from one vendor within days, each just under an approver limit, sum above it. Limits are constants in code keyed by `employees.role`. Decoy: fixed monthly fee under a contract |
| `revenue_inflation` | part of round trip | **New.** Sales invoices (issuer = company) with revenue in the ledger and no bank inflow within N days, or `status = cancelado` without a ledger reversal, concentrated at period end to a fresh RFC |
| — | `duplicate_invoice_payment` | Not in their enum. Keep the detectors; report a hit as a **control-failure observation** in the declined-leads section (`closed_by: validator`, reason states it is outside the five types), never as a finding |

## Output mapping

| Judges' field | Source in our pipeline |
|---|---|
| `entities` (`RFC:…`, `EMP:…`) | accused ids, already prefixed by the adapter |
| `exhibits[≥3]` `{exhibit_id, source_table, record_id, note}` | finding evidence + `ds.record_table[id]`; notes from a per-kind template ("invoice from X to company, no PO") |
| `money_trail[]` `{from, to, amount, date, exhibit_id}` | bank_txns rows among the evidence, ordered by date; from/to as entity names |
| `peso_amount` | sum of cited invoice totals (or cited txn amounts), per table, ±2 % |
| `confidence` | `proven` when every rule precondition has an exhibit, else `probable` |
| `leads_not_pursued[]` `{entity, signal, reason, tool_calls_made, closed_by}` | dossier detectors → `signal`; #69 checks → `reason`; step log → `tool_calls_made`; investigator / challenger / validator |
| `run_metadata` | counter in `LLM.chat` (calls, tokens), `MXN_PER_1K_TOKENS` constant, `wall_s` from the log, `deterministic: true` (cache + T=0 + seed) |
| Case file sections 1–5 | `agent/report.py` rewrite; HTML export with inline SVG money trail |

## What we cut, and say so

- Employee home address as a kickback tell (not in their schema).
- Goods receipts and counterparty statements as separate tables (folded into POs/contracts and `bank_txns`).
- The "Desvirtuado" decoy (their `efos_list` has two statuses).
- `duplicate_invoice_payment` as a finding type (reported as a control observation).
- Tax-exposure figures stay in the report as a bonus, never in `peso_amount`.

## Questions to ask Richard or Koss today

1. Will judge estates arrive as a SQLite `.db`, CSVs, or both? (We will read both.)
2. Do their `bank_txns` include third-party legs (vendor → employee, vendor → customer), or only the company's own transfers? This decides whether round-tripping and kickbacks are provable from bank data or must rest on approver and CLABE links.
3. Are approval limits given anywhere in the estate, or should we state ours as constants?
4. Is an accusation for a control failure outside the five types (a double payment) wanted anywhere in the submission?

## Why not stay the course

Staying means our numbers are perfect on our own schema and unmeasurable on theirs. The README is explicit
that scoring happens on their estates, at a path we receive at run time, with a fixed scheme enum and a
format validator they will run. The core of the build is right; the edges must change, and the adapter
makes that a bounded job rather than a rewrite.
